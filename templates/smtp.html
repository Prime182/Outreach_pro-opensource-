SMTP Configuration Help

Common SMTP Settings

  • Gmail: Host: smtp.gmail.com, Port: 587 (TLS)
  • Yahoo: Host: smtp.mail.yahoo.com, Port: 587 (TLS)
  • Outlook/Hotmail: Host: smtp.office365.com, Port: 587 (TLS)

Setting Up Gmail For Sending

  1. Enable 2-Factor Authentication on your Google account
  2. Generate an App Password (Google Account → Security → App Passwords)
  3. Use your Gmail address as the username and the App Password as the password

Important Note About Sender Email

Most email providers require that the sender email address matches the authenticated account. This is why your SMTP username (typically your email address) will be used as the sender address in campaigns. Your display name will still appear as shown to recipients (e.g., "Your Name <your.email@example.com>").
